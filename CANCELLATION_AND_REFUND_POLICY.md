# BotFlow AI – Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026

At BotFlow AI, we aim to provide high-performance conversational AI tooling for your business. We want you to be completely satisfied with your subscription. This Cancellation and Refund Policy describes how cancellations and refunds are handled for paid plans (Pro and Business) processed via Razorpay.

---

## 1. Subscription Cancellation

### How to Cancel
You can cancel your BotFlow AI paid subscription at any time directly through your account dashboard:
1. Log into your BotFlow AI account.
2. Click your organization initials in the top navigation and open **Billing & Plans** (or click the Billing button in the dashboard).
3. Under your current active plan, click **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Confirm cancellation.

### Effect of Cancellation
- **Retained Access:** When you cancel, your subscription will **not** be terminated immediately. You will retain full access to all features, chatbots, documents, and message quotas corresponding to your paid tier until the end of the current billing cycle (`current_period_end`).
- **No Further Charges:** Once cancelled, your Razorpay recurring auto-debit will be cancelled immediately, and no future charges will occur.
- **Graceful Downgrade to Free Tier:** At the end of the paid billing period, your account will automatically revert to the **Free Plan** (1 Chatbot, 100 messages/month, 3 documents). Your existing chatbots and documents exceeding free tier limits will remain intact in read-only mode until upgraded or deleted.

---

## 2. Refund Policy

### 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee (New Subscriptions)
We offer a **no-questions-asked 7-day money-back guarantee** on your first paid subscription cycle:
- If you are unsatisfied with BotFlow AI for any reason within seven (7) calendar days of your initial upgrade to a Pro or Business plan, contact `billing@botflow.ai` or submit a refund request from your dashboard.
- We will issue a full refund (100%) of the subscription fee paid for that initial billing cycle.

### Annual Subscriptions Pro-Rata Refund
- If you purchased an **Annual Plan** and decide to cancel within thirty (30) days of purchase, you are eligible for a pro-rata refund minus the standard monthly rate for the elapsed period.

### Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds are not granted in the following scenarios:
1. Refund requests submitted after the 7-day initial money-back window for monthly plans.
2. Subscriptions terminated due to serious violations of our **Acceptable Use Policy** (e.g. prompt injection abuse, phishing, illegal distribution, malware).
3. Failure to cancel before the automatic renewal date where the service was actively utilized throughout the billing period.

---

## 3. How Refunds are Processed

- **Payment Gateway:** All refunds are processed back to the original payment source (credit card, debit card, UPI, or Net Banking) via our official payment partner **Razorpay**.
- **Processing Timeline:** Once approved by our team, refunds are initiated immediately in Razorpay. Funds typically appear in your bank or card statement within **5 to 7 business days**, subject to your issuing bank's clearing cycle.
- **Confirmation:** You will receive a Razorpay refund confirmation email with a unique `rfnd_` reference identifier for tracking.

---

## 4. Disputed Charges & Inquiries

If you notice an unexpected or duplicate charge, please contact our billing team before filing a bank chargeback:
- **Billing Support Email:** billing@botflow.ai
- **General Support:** support@botflow.ai
- **Phone / Helpdesk:** Available within the dashboard for Business tier subscribers.

We resolve billing inquiries and dispute investigations within 24 business hours.
